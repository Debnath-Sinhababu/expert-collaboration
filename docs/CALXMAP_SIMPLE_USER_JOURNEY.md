# Calxmap — Pura Safar, Seedhe Shabd Mein

**Yeh document kiske liye hai:** Koi bhi jo Calxmap ka naya flow samajhna chahta ho — bina kisi
technical language ke. Ek hi real example se poora safar samjhayenge.

**Example hum yeh lenge:**

| Kaun | Kaun hai |
|---|---|
| **Meera Joshi** | Trainer. Mumbai mein rehti hai. Gen AI padhati hai. Calxmap par pehli baar aa rahi hai. |
| **Prof. Kulkarni** | Sinhgad Institute, Pune. Final year ke 120 students ke liye training chahiye. |
| **Calxmap ki madad** | Ek smart assistant jo dono ki taraf se kaam karta hai — message bhejna, match karna, yaad dilana. |

**Unka kaam kya hai:** Pune campus par 2 din ki Gen AI training — 8 aur 9 September ko. Har din
lagbhag 8 ghante. College ₹20,000 dega. Meera ko milega ₹14,000.

---

## Pehle ek baat clear karo — do alag baatcheet

Bahut log isme confuse ho jaate hain. Meera se **do alag baar** baat hogi — alag kaam ke liye:

| | Pehli baat (chhoti) | Doosri baat (thodi lambi) |
|---|---|---|
| **Kab** | Resume ke turant baad | Profile poora hone ke baad |
| **Kitni der** | 2–3 minute | 12–15 minute |
| **Kaise** | Chat / WhatsApp par likh kar | Phone par baat karke (voice) |
| **Kyun** | Rate, availability, travel — yeh resume mein hota hi nahi | Sach mein padha sakti hai ya nahi — yeh check karna |
| **Fail ho sakti hai?** | Nahi — bas details bharni hain | Haan — agar score kam aaya |

**Yaad rakho:** Pehli baat = form bharna jaisa. Doosri baat = asli interview jaisa.

## Hissa 1 — Meera Calxmap par aati hai (10 din pehle)

### Step 1: Resume daalo, baaki hum karenge

Meera website kholti hai. Use lambi form nahi milti. Sirf ek cheez:

> *"Apna resume upload karo — baaki hum bharenge. 30 second lagenge."*

Wo apna 2-page resume daalti hai. Calxmap ki madad usme se padh leti hai:
- Naam, phone, email, sheher
- Kitne saal ka experience
- Kahan kaam kiya
- Kaunsi skills likhi hain — Gen AI, Python, Prompt Engineering

**Lekin seedha save nahi hota.** Meera ko ek screen dikhti hai jahan sab kuch likha hota hai aur
wo confirm karti hai:

> *"Humne yeh bhara hai — ek baar dekh lo. Sahi hai?"*

Agar kuch galat ho — jaise experience 8 saal ki jagah 3 dikhe — wo theek kar leti hai. **Yeh step
kabhi skip nahi hoga.** Resume hamesha thoda galat padh sakta hai.

---

### Step 2: Teen minute ki chat — rate aur availability

Resume mein teen cheezein **kabhi nahi hoti**, aur bina inke matching nahi ho sakta:

1. Kitna charge karti ho ek session ke liye?
2. Kaunse din free rehti ho?
3. Mumbai se bahar travel karogi? Pune tak?

Isliye Calxmap ki madad seedhe poochti hai:

> **Madad:** Ek session (6–8 ghante) ke liye kitna charge karti ho?
> **Meera:** 6000
> **Madad:** Isse kam par kaam karogi? Minimum?
> **Meera:** 5000 se kam nahi
> **Madad:** September mein kab free ho?
> **Meera:** Tuesday Wednesday free rehta hai
> **Madad:** Pune tak travel theek hai?
> **Meera:** Haan

Kabhi-kabhi ek chhota sawal aur — jaise *"TCS mein kya karti thi?"* — yeh sirf check ke liye hai
ki resume sach ka hai ya kisi aur ka daal diya.

**Poora step chat mein hota hai.** Phone par call ki zaroorat nahi — bas type karo, jawab do.

---

### Step 3: Skill check — 15 minute ki voice interview

Ab Meera ki profile ready hai, lekin abhi tak wo **"Verified" nahi** hai. Screen par likha aata hai:

> *"Verified experts ko 3 guna zyada kaam milta hai. 15 minute ka interview do."*

Meera start karti hai. Pehle bataya jata hai:

> *"Yeh ek robot assistant hai, insaan nahi. Call record hogi. Aage badhne ke liye haan bolo."*

6–7 sawal — Gen AI ke baare mein, aur ek sawal jaise *"120 students ko bina coding background ke
kaise samjhaogi?"*

**Result:** Score 82/100 — Strong. Ab Meera **Verified** hai. Ek baar hua, phir mahino tak chalega.

---

### Agar yahan kuch galat ho jaye? (Meera ke onboarding ke edge cases)

| Agar yeh ho | Toh kya hoga |
|---|---|
| Resume photo hai, padh nahi sakte | Madad chat se hi sab pooch legi |
| Resume Hindi/Marathi mein hai | Waise hi padhne ki koshish; nahi hua toh chat se bharega |
| Resume mein kuch galat padh liya | Review screen par Meera theek karegi — bina confirm ke save nahi |
| Resume mein aisi skill jo list mein nahi | Sabse paas wala option suggest hoga |
| Resume hi nahi hai | Thodi lambi chat se profile ban jayegi |
| Kisi aur ka resume daal diya | Chhote sawalon se pakda jayega; skill interview mein aur clear ho jayega |
| Beech mein chhod diya signup | 24 aur 72 ghante baad ek reminder; uske baad chup |
| Skill interview mein fail | Ban nahi hogi. 7 din baad ek aur chance. Teesri baar — insaan dekhega |
| Internet kharab, awaaz nahi aa rahi | Dobara schedule hoga — technical problem par fail nahi |
| Score se disagree | Transcript dekh kar insaan review karega |
| Nayi skill baad mein add ki | Sirf us skill ke liye chhota interview — poora dubara nahi |
| Purana verification expire ho gaya | 18 mahine baad chhota refresh interview |
| Same phone se dobara account | Pehle wala account merge karne ka option |
| WhatsApp nahi hai | Email/SMS se kaam chalega; ranking thodi kam |
| Message bhejne ki permission nahi di | Sirf zaroori email — WhatsApp nahi |

## Hissa 2 — Prof. Kulkarni apni zaroorat batata hai (8 September se 2 din pehle)

### Koi form nahi — seedhe baat

Prof. Kulkarni Calxmap kholte hain aur likhte hain:

> *"Final year students ke liye Gen AI training chahiye. 2 sessions, har ek 8 ghante. Campus par
> offline. 8–9 September. Budget ₹10,000 per session."*

Calxmap ki madad confirm karti hai:

> *"Samajh gaya — Gen AI · 2 sessions · 8 ghante each · Pune campus · 8–9 Sept · Total ₹20,000 ·
> Expert ko ₹14,000 milega. Kitne students?"*

> **Prof:** 120 students.

> **Madad:** Ho gaya. **48 ghante mein shortlist** milegi. Ab aapko kuch nahi karna.

Prof. Kulkarni ka kaam yahan khatam — 2 din wait karna hai.

---

### Agar requirement mein problem ho?

| Agar yeh ho | Toh kya hoga |
|---|---|
| Bahut vague likha — "AI training chahiye" | 3–4 sawal aur; usse zyada nahi — warna team se baat hogi |
| Budget bahut kam hai | Turant bataya jayega — "Is skill ke liye log itna lete hain, aapka budget kam hai" — options diye jayenge |
| Kal se training chahiye | Pehle hi bola jayega — shortlist + interview + sign karne mein 5 din lagte hain |
| Beech mein requirement badal di | Chhoti cheez (student count) — chalega. Badi cheez (date, budget, skill) — dubara shuru, purane experts ko bataya jayega |
| Same requirement do baar daal di | System puchega — merge karna hai? |
| Ek program ke liye 3 trainer chahiye | Alag-alag shortlist — har trainer ke liye alag letter |

## Hissa 3 — Matching aur WhatsApp message (uske 5 minute baad)

Calxmap ki madad andar se dekhti hai:
- Kaun verified hai Gen AI mein?
- Kaun ₹7,000 per session par haan bolega? (Meera ka minimum ₹5,000 hai — offer ₹7,000/session)
- Kaun Pune travel kar sakta hai?
- Kaun 8–9 Sept par free hai?

11 log match hote hain. Meera **#2** par hai.

Top 6 ko WhatsApp aata hai:

> **Calxmap**
>
> Gen AI training · Pune campus · 2 sessions × 8 ghante · **8–9 September**
> Aapko milega: **₹14,000** (₹7,000/session)
>
> **[Haan, interested] [Nahi] [Aur batao]**

**Yahan koi rate ki baatcheet nahi hoti.** Price fix hai. Sirf haan ya nahi.

Meera 20 minute mein **Haan** dabati hai.

Do aur log haan bolte hain. Ek nahi. Do chup rehte hain — unhe 24 ghante baad reminder, phir agle
rank wale ko message.

---

### Matching ke edge cases

| Agar yeh ho | Toh kya hoga |
|---|---|
| Koi expert match hi nahi | 2 ghante mein college ko sach bataya jayega — date badlo, budget badhao, ya online karo |
| Sirf 1–2 log mile | Chhoti shortlist — **kharaab log se bharna nahi** — sach bolenge "sirf 2 mile" |
| Haan bola, phir gayab ho gaya | Agla rank wala automatic aayega; reliability score gir jayega |
| Sabne nahi kaha | Team ko alert; college ko options |
| Ek hi expert do overlapping kaam mein | Pehle confirm hone wala jeetega; doosre ko bataya jayega kyun nahi |
| Beech mein rate badal di expert ne | Invite ke time jo rate tha wahi maana jayega |
| Shortlist ke baad available nahi rahi | Shortlist se hata di; college ko bataya; agla rank aayega |
| Bahut zyada message aa rahe expert ko | Hafte mein limit — spam nahi hoga |

## Hissa 4 — Meera ka WhatsApp message — availability bhi yahi confirm hoti hai

**Yeh bahut important hai.** Pehle experts ko alag se calendar bharna padta tha — wo update nahi
karte the, aur galat lagta tha ki wo busy hain.

Ab **"interested ho?" aur "in dates par free ho?" — ek hi sawal hai.**

Meera ne **Haan** dabaya — iska matlab:
- 8 aur 9 September par free hai
- ₹14,000 par haam hai
- Pune ja sakti hai

Agar wo **"Nahi, 9 nahi — 10–11 kar sakti hoon"** bolti, toh madad samajh kar college ko option
deti: *"Meera 10–11 Sept par available hai — chalega?"*

**Calendar maintain karne ki zaroorat nahi.** Sirf jab opportunity aaye tab ek tap.

---

### Availability — agar kuch ulta ho?

| Agar yeh ho | Toh kya hoga |
|---|---|
| Expert ne kabhi calendar nahi bhara | **Busy nahi dikhega** — "abhi confirm ho raha hai" dikhega |
| Haan bola, baad mein kahi aur book ho gaya | Select hone se pehle ek aur baar poocha jayega — "ab bhi confirm?" |
| Do colleges ne ek hi expert ko chuna | Pehle letter sign karne wala jeetega; doosre ko agla option |
| Bahut din ho gaye, college ne select nahi kiya | 14 din baad dubara poocha jayega — "ab bhi 8–9 Sept theek?" |
| Pune session hai, pehle din travel chahiye | System khud pehle din shaam aur baad wale din subah block kar dega |
| Sirf pehle din free, doosre din nahi | College ko choice — do trainer ya ek din reschedule |
| College ne date badal di | Sabko dubara poocha jayega |
| Free text mein likha "10 chalega 9 nahi" | Madad wapas puchegi — "10 Sept confirm hai?" — bina confirm kiye aage nahi |
| Rolling confirm ka jawab nahi aaya | 2 baar ke baad team ko bataya jayega |

## Hissa 6 — Interview (3 September, shaam 4 baje)

Prof. Kulkarni **"Pehle interview karo"** dabate hain.

**Lekin slot pehle se fix hai** — Meera ne invite ke time hi 3 Sept, 4 PM choose kar liya tha.
Koi phone tag nahi, koi "kab free ho?" ka round nahi.

Dono ko link aata hai. Join karte hain. Pehle consent:

> *"Yeh call record hogi. Personal number share karna allowed nahi. Haan bolo toh aage badho."*

22 minute par Prof. bolte hain:

> *"Meera, aage bhi kaam hoga — apna number de do, seedhe baat karte hain—"*

**2 second baad mic band ho jata hai.** Screen par warning:

> ⚠️ *Contact details share karna allowed nahi. Mic 10 second ke liye band.*

Poora number nahi nikal paaya — 2 digit sunai diye, baaki nahi. **Yeh asli rok hai, sirf record
nahi.**

Call khatam. Dono khush.

---

### Interview ke edge cases

| Agar yeh ho | Toh kya hoga |
|---|---|
| Ek side nahi aayi | 10 minute wait, phir cancel + reschedule link; miss karne wale ka score gir jayega |
| Contact share karne ki koshish | Pehli baar warning → phir mic band → phir call khatam → baar-baar toh account review |
| Galat alarm — "call me Meera" jaise normal baat | Pehli baar sirf warning, penalty nahi |
| Hindi/Marathi mein number bola | System samajhta hai — sirf English nahi |
| Screen par card dikhaya | Screen band ho jayegi |
| College ne galat sawal pucha (jaati, dharm, umar) | Bot warning dega — expert ki safety |
| Expert ne call mein zyada paise maange | Bot yaad dilayega — rate fix hai |
| Internet beech mein gaya | Audio chalega, 10 minute andar wapas join |
| 3 experts ka interview chahiye | Ek ke baad ek slot |
| Recording ki permission nahi | Platform par call nahi — ya toh consent do ya bina interview select karo |

## Hissa 7 — Select, letter, sign (3 September, shaam 6 baje)

Prof. Kulkarni **Select** dabate hain.

**Ab kya hota hai — step by step:**

1. Meera ko ek message: *"Sinhgad Institute ne aapko chuna. 8–9 Sept ab bhi confirm hai?"*
   Meera ek tap mein **Haan** bolti hai.

2. **Offer letter** ban kar Meera ke app mein aata hai — ₹14,000, dates, saari terms.

3. Meera padhti hai, apna naam type karti hai, date likhti hai, **Accept** dabati hai.

4. Signed copy college aur admin ko email hoti hai.

5. **Ab poora naam, photo, contact college ko dikhta hai** — kyunki ab kaam shuru hone wala hai,
   coordination chahiye.

6. Booking confirm — training ki taiyari.

---

### Letter aur signing ke edge cases

| Agar yeh ho | Toh kya hoga |
|---|---|
| Meera ne letter reject kar diya | College ko shortlist wapas — baaki 3 cards ab bhi hain; koi uljhan nahi |
| 3 din mein jawab nahi | Auto-expire; agla shortlisted expert ko offer |
| Sign karte waqt naam thoda alag likha | Confirmation popup — "Yeh naam sahi hai?" |
| Select ke baad date badalni hai | Naya letter, expert dubara sign karega — chupke se edit nahi |
| College ne cancel kar diya | Policy ke hisaab se compensation; expert ko turant bataya jayega |
| Expert ka verification pending tha | Thodi der hold; resolve nahi hua toh agla option |

## Hissa 10 — Agar program lamba ho? (2 mahine ka example — same Meera, alag college)

Ab ek chhota twist — taaki samajh aaye jab dates fix nahi hoti.

Socho **MIT College** ko 1 October se 30 November tak Gen AI program chahiye. Exact din pata nahi —
*"mostly Saturday ko hoga, baad mein fix karenge."*

**Yahan kya alag hoga:**

**College bolta hai:**
> *"Do mahine ka program, October–November, lagbhag 16 sessions, exact schedule baad mein."*

**Meera ko message:**
> *"1 Oct – 30 Nov tak program hai. Saturday subah sessions ho sakti hain. ~₹1,12,000 total.
> Is range mein commit kar sakti ho?"*
>
> **[Haan] [Nahi] [Kuch dates block hain]**

Meera bolti hai Diwali week (20–27 Oct) block hai. Agent confirm karata hai:
> *"Saturday subah, max 6 sessions/month, 20–27 Oct blocked — sahi?"*

**Shortlist par dikhega:** "Saturday AM · Oct–Nov confirm · Diwali blocked" — **60 din ka calendar
grid nahi.**

**Booking ke baad — har Monday:**
College batata hai: *"Agle 2 hafte 4 Oct aur 11 Oct session rakho."*
Meera ko message: *"4 Oct aur 11 Oct confirm?"* — ek tap.

**Matlab:** Lambe program mein pehle **pattern** confirm hota hai (Saturday subah), baad mein **har
hafte actual dates** — calendar maintain nahi karna padta.

## Hissa 11 — Identity chhupana — simple words mein

**Pehle booking confirm hone se:**
- College dekhe: Meera J., Mumbai, skills, score, rating, fees
- College **nahi** dekhe: poora naam, phone, email, LinkedIn, photo

**Booking confirm hone ke baad:**
- Sab kuch dikhe — kyunki ab saath mein kaam karna hai

**Teen cheezein dhyaan se:**

1. **Resume download** — pehle booking se pehle resume nahi milega (ya naam-phone hata kar milega)
2. **Profile video** — pehle nahi dikhega
3. **Interview recording** — college ko sirf summary milegi, poori recording nahi

**Interview room mein bhi naam "Meera J." dikhega** — warna invite se hi poora naam leak ho jayega.

**Sach baat:** Koi bahut ziddi ho toh video se pehchan sakta hai. Isliye letter mein likha hai ki
platform ke bahar deal karna allowed nahi — aur call record proof hai.

## Hissa 12 — Hum kya automatically nahi karenge

Kuch cheezein hamesha insaan ya fixed rule se hi hongi — chahe kitni bhi smart system ho:

- Offer letter likhna aur sign karwana
- Attendance ke baad payment release
- Koi dispute ho toh sulah
- Identity documents check (KYC)
- Tax, invoice, paise transfer
- Har action ka record rakhna — "kisne kab kya kiya"

Yeh safety ke liye hai. Paise aur legal cheezon par robot ko akela chhodna safe nahi.

## Ant mein — yeh naya flow kyun?

**Purana flow:** Bahut saare steps, bahut saare buttons, har step par kuch ulajh sakta tha. Expert
apply kare, wait kare, interview stage, rate baatcheet, phir offer — har jagah alag screen.

**Naya flow:**
- College bolta hai kya chahiye — bas
- System dhundhta hai log — college wait karta hai
- Expert ko ek message — haan/nahi
- Shortlist — ek tap select
- Letter sign — booking

**Ulajhan peeche chhupi hai, saamne sirf kaam dikhta hai.**
